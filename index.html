Overview

Status Grab is a utility application developed by AandK Tech that allows users to view, save, and share WhatsApp statuses (photos and videos) directly from their Android device.

We are deeply committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we access, why we access it, and how we handle it. By using Status Grab, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

🔒 Short Version: Status Grab works entirely on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers.
📊

Data We Collect

Status Grab is designed with a privacy-first approach. Here is a complete breakdown of what we do and do not collect:

  • We do NOT collect your name, email, or phone number
  • We do NOT collect location data of any kind
  • We do NOT read, access, or upload your WhatsApp messages or contacts
  • We do NOT share any data with third-party advertisers (ads are currently disabled)
  • We do NOT upload your photos or videos to any server
  • App preferences (e.g., dark/light theme) are stored locally on your device only
  • Saved status files remain only on your device in your chosen folder
🔑

⚙️

How We Use Data

All data processing in Status Grab happens locally on your device. Here is exactly what each feature does:

  • Status Browser: Reads WhatsApp status files from local device storage and displays them within the app only
  • Save Status: Copies selected media to your chosen folder on your device — no upload, no server
  • Share Status: Uses Android's native share sheet to share files via apps you choose (e.g., WhatsApp, Telegram)
  • Saved Status Gallery: Displays previously saved files from your local device folder
  • Theme Preference: Stores dark/light mode preference locally using SharedPreferences
  • App Settings: All settings are stored entirely on-device, never synced externally

Children's Privacy

Status Grab is designed for users aged 16 and above, as WhatsApp itself requires users to be at least 13–16 years old depending on the region.

We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has used our app and provided personal information, please contact us immediately and we will take appropriate steps.

💽

Data Storage & Security

All data generated by Status Grab — including saved media files and app preferences — is stored exclusively on your local device.

  • No user data is transmitted to AandK Tech servers or any third-party servers
  • Saved status files are stored in a folder you grant access to, on your own device
  • App preferences are stored using Android's SharedPreferences (local, encrypted by the OS)
  • You can delete all app data via Android Settings → Apps → Status Grab → Clear Data
⚖️

Your Rights

Since Status Grab does not collect personal data on external servers, most traditional data rights are fulfilled automatically — all your data stays on your device. You also have the following rights:

  • Revoke any permission at any time via Android Settings → Apps → Status Grab → Permissions
  • Delete all saved status files from your device storage at any time
  • Uninstall the app to remove all app preferences and cached data
  • Contact us at any time regarding privacy concerns
